## What the license covers

It covers Source Foshan's selection and arrangement of the codes, the product examples, search
aliases (English and Chinese), "choose when" conditions, classification notes and the guidance
rules.

The `officialDescription` fields quote the World Customs Organization's Harmonized System
nomenclature (HS 2022). That text remains subject to the WCO's own terms and is not relicensed here.

## No warranty

The data is reference information, not a customs ruling. National tariff lines (8–10 digits) and
duty rates differ by country. Confirm any classification with a licensed customs broker or the
customs authority of the destination country.
